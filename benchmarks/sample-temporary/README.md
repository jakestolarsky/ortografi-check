experiment (original, no third-party data). They contain a few deliberate errors
(typos, missing/unnecessary commas). They are **not annotated**, not reviewed by a Polish
punctuation expert, and must not be used to judge quality.

Replace with the Ortografi Corpus once its format is delivered (PLAN.md section 10).
